Woodland Attire

We invite you to dress in Forest Fantasy or Semi-Formal Garden attire.

  • The Palette: Earthy tones, moss greens, deep burgundies, autumn gold, soft creams, and twilight blues.
  • Footwear: You will be walking on grass, dirt paths, and stone steps. Sturdy wedges, flats, boots, or dress shoes fit for a woodland stroll are highly recommended.
  • Sunshine: You may need a hat or umbrella for shade from the sun.
  • Layers: The forest air will grow cool after sunset. We suggest bringing a cloak, shawl, or warm jacket for later.

Afternoon tea & bubbles

After the ceremony join us for catered afternoon tea and drinks.

  • Fine delights: A rustic afternoon tea buffet.
  • Liquid refreshments: Bubbles and other cold and hot drinks will be available throughout the afternoon.

The Potluck Feast

In the evening we will be joined for dinner and live music by the afterparty crew. Bring a plate to share inspired by a Shire bounty and Elven hospitality.

  • First Supper & Mains: A rustic, farm-to-table potluck buffet featuring artisanal breads, roasted meats, seasonal greens, and root vegetables.
  • Libations: Ale, wines, and botanical mocktails will flow throughout the evening.
